Independent Researcher — AI Evaluation Infrastructure

May 2026 – present · Austin, TX

Measurement on the measurers: validate closed models at the boundary; certify open models from inside the stack; test whether validity survives the copy.

Closed models — is the score valid?

Automating the dissertation's MEV framework into a self-validating text-to-measurement instrument for constrained LLMs (fixed output space, temperature 0, era-grounded exemplars), checking every score against blinded exogenous benchmarks with falsifiable verdicts (validated, refinable, misspecified); deployed beyond its native survey domain onto congressional floor speech to generate an affective measure for Congress over time. Offline tier validated at n = 1200 (r = 0.70), with injected drift correctly downgrading the verdict. The affect measure demonstrates the instrument; the instrument is the contribution.

Copy validity. Distilled the dissertation's validated GPT-4 judge into open-weight models (Qwen, Mistral): the copies matched the teacher's scores at r = 0.74 yet recovered only 0.54 validity against held-out feeling thermometers, versus the teacher's 0.70 — agreement certifies the copy, not the judge. Extended into a 29-judge ladder trained under experimenter control (two model families, three sizes, three data doses, mid-training snapshots, a scrambled-label control) to test whether teacher-agreement is a sufficient statistic for criterion validity. The judges demonstrate the method; what makes it evidence is the regime below.

Open-weight models — certified in two halves.

White box — the measuring half: is the score stable?

Stability. A purpose-built reliability harness — when the measurement layer is itself the object of study, the instrument cannot inherit untested machinery — testing whether identically configured temperature-0 evaluation ever disagrees with itself, and pricing every condition that makes it disagree. Four open-weight models (Qwen2.5-0.5B/7B, SmolLM2-360M, Mistral-7B), frozen hash-verified prompt suites, five to ten cold-start replications per configuration — 108 hash-verified runs — spanning CPU/GPU execution, precision formats, batching, threading, autotuned kernels, and tensor-parallel inference on pinned NVIDIA A100s. Every replication was bit-for-bit identical; variance appears only between configurations, four orders of magnitude apart with precision format dominant — number format alone moves declared MMLU accuracy by up to four points in either direction, and fp16 silently destroyed 99.5% of one 7B model's token probabilities, perfectly reproducibly, while sparing its equal-sized cross-architecture twin. The bitwise-zero baseline doubles as a tripwire for silent environment change. In progress: declared-randomness certification — at temperature 0.7 under declared seeds, the floor generalized to an envelope computed from the deterministic runs' own logits.

Glass box — the proving half: what can an outsider check?

One regime governs both instruments. Predictions are sealed before any run — eighteen hypotheses under named falsification conditions for the harness, SHA-256 hash-chained across eight successive preregistrations, each version provably containing its predecessor; six fell, and the falsifications are published as the findings. The judge ladder is that discipline in its sharpest form: a sealed single-read design with an air-gapped criterion, mechanical verdicts, and control gates that bind the experimenter before results are seen. Provenance is read from the machine, not from documentation: every harness run emits a self-hashing manifest whose condition vector — precision format, kernels, determinism flags, silicon — is read back from live system state on every execution — continuous monitoring, not point-in-time certification. Every ruling lands verbatim in an append-only decision log: the dated, attributed test records EU AI Act Annex IV §2(g) describes, aligned to NIST AI RMF record-keeping. MIT-licensed, CI-verified, and built to be re-derivable by an independent party on commodity hardware — the check a second-line reviewer or external auditor could actually run.
