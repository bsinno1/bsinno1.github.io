Measurement Instruments

open-weight-eval-reliability-harness MIT

Measurement-science harness for LLM evaluation reliability: the noise floor of temperature-0 evaluation on open-weight models, with per-knob attribution mapped to EU AI Act Art. 15 and NIST AI RMF MEASURE 2/3. Frozen hash-verified probe set, hypotheses hashed before any run, five to ten replications per configuration. Every replication came back bit-for-bit identical; variance lives between configurations, not within them.

Python Behavioral layer of the open-model certification work
barea-dissertation Dataset MIT / CC-BY-4.0

Prompting and analysis pipelines for the dissertation's two measurement chapters — partisan affect and national economic ideology on ANES survey text, 1984–2020 — together with the measures they produce.

The released measures. data/ holds partisan affect scores (10,759 scored observations across 12 ANES waves, emitted on the feeling-thermometer scale alongside the thermometers they were validated against), the national economic ideology index over seven CAP policy domains across 11 waves, the 660-response human gold standard, and the rater-by-rater agreement table behind the reported statistics. The codebook documents the coding, the sampling design that puts some respondents on more than one row, and the internal consistency of the seven-domain index (mean inter-domain r = 0.53, α = 0.88).

What is not there. ANES's own data products are not mirrored. To reproduce from the beginning you need the ANES Time Series Cumulative Data File and the open-ended verbatim releases from ANES (free account) — see docs/DATA.md. Nothing else needs downloading. Code is MIT, the datasets are CC-BY-4.0, and the ANES variables carried inside them stay subject to ANES's terms. No API credentials appear in the tree or its history.

political-polarization-NAACL Fork Dataset MIT

Code and annotated corpus for "Political ideology and polarization of policy positions: A multi-dimensional approach" (NAACL 2022) — the first diachronic dataset of news articles annotated at paragraph level for ideology by trained political scientists and linguists.

The dataset. 333 gold-annotated articles (gold_train 297 / gold_dev 18 / gold_test 18). Each article carries an author-stance label plus per-paragraph labels on three ideological dimensions — fiscal, social, and foreign — for up to 12 paragraphs, coded left-leaning, right-leaning, mixed (both present), or not relevant to the dimension. That is 5,346 paragraph-label cells in the training split alone, evenly split across the three dimensions. A companion file supplies year, publication, and original plus segmented text for 579 articles, joined on article ID. Annotating the author's stance separately from each paragraph is what lets ideological position be measured while controlling for who is speaking.

Unmodified fork of bernovie/political-polarization @ 38a61f2 · MIT, © 2022 Bernardo Oviedo Paper Data README
adaptive-ensembling Fork Dataset

Code and datasets for "Adaptive ensembling: Unsupervised domain adaptation for political document analysis" (EMNLP 2019).

The release includes our COCA political-category annotations: datasets/coca_train.csv and coca_test.csv label each document for American Government, Political Economy, and International Relations (a document with no positive label is non-political), and coca_politics.txt lists the document identifiers our domain-adaptation framework labels political. Annotations are keyed to COCA document IDs rather than shipping the source text, so COCA access is needed to join them.

Unmodified fork of shreydesai/adaptive-ensembling @ 05e6a76 Paper
venkatasg/interpersonal-bias Dataset MIT

Code and data for "How people talk about each other: Modeling generalized intergroup bias and emotion" (EACL 2023).

The dataset. 3,033 tweets by members of Congress, each annotated for eight interpersonal emotions (admiration, anger, disgust, fear, interest, joy, sadness, surprise) together with the speaker's in-group/out-group relation to the person mentioned and the party of each. The full annotation protocol ships with it. Following platform terms the release lists tweet IDs only — contact the authors for the hydrated text.

Maintained by coauthor Venkata S. Govindarajan (not a fork of mine) Paper
